- **Second-order chain:** if insurers take a profit hit, they will cut extra benefits and push harder on drug costs, including weight-loss drugs. That is a speculative, indirect risk for Lilly.
- **Horizon:** to mid-January (short-term) and months (structural).
- **What would invalidate it:** CMS dropping extrapolation, or a final rule tougher than proposed.
